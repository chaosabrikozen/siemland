SIEM review

A fixed-price review of your SIEM, in two parts.

Log ingestion

Are the right logs arriving, complete and on time? The review checks for the problems found most often:

  • Clocks out of step: sources without proper time sync (NTP) or with the wrong time zone, and the time an event happened correlated with the time the SIEM received it.
    Example from a report
    SOURCEEVENT TIME (UTC)RECEIVED (UTC)OFFSET
    fw-edge-0108:41:0208:41:031 s
    dc-0207:41:0508:41:061 h, time zone
    app-db-0308:39:5808:41:0668 s, no NTP
  • Sources gone quiet: servers and devices that stopped sending logs. This is more serious than usually admitted: the rules that depend on them stop triggering, and nothing warns anyone.
    Example from a report
    SOURCELAST EVENTUSUALLY SENDSRULES AFFECTED
    vpn-gw-016 days agoevery minute14
    dc-0719 hours agoevery second9
    proxy-012 minutes agoevery second0
  • Logs not parsed: events in CEF over TCP, CEF over Syslog, plain Syslog, key/value pairs, JSON or vendor formats stored as raw text, or with fields mapped wrongly, so rules cannot read them.
    Example from a report
    SOURCEFORMATFIELDS PARSEDSTATUS
    fw-edge-01CEF over TCP28 of 28ok
    proxy-01CEF over Syslog4 of 31mostly raw
    erp-appkey/value0 of 22raw text
  • Gaps: important systems never connected, or connected only in part.
